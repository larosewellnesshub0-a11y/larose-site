# Reference analysis — Fakeeh Care (en.dsfhjeddah.fakeeh.care)

Analysed 2026-09-05 in Chrome. The group site (`en.fakeeh.care`) is corporate only;
the **branch hospital sites** carry the structure worth copying. 376 URLs in the sitemap.

## 1. Sitemap shape (what a mature hospital site actually contains)

| Section | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `/doctor/{slug}` | 179 | one page per doctor — the site's real mass |
| `/specialties/{slug}` | 44 | one page per specialty + a hub |
| `/specialty-doctors/{slug}` | 43 | a *filtered doctor list per specialty* — separate URL |
| `/about-us/*` | 27 | incl. `stories-of-success/*` (10 case stories), awards, management, podcasts, articles-blogs |
| `/facilities/*` | 15 | units: ICU, NICU, endoscopy, pharmacy, diagnostics, robotic surgery |
| `/plan-your-visit/*` | 9 | admission guide, FAQ, insurance & billing, patient guide, rights & responsibilities, wayfinding |
| `/clinics/*` | 6 | the branch/medical-centre network |
| standalone | ~15 | health-packages, laboratory-packages, home-care, medication-delivery, virtual-consultation, educational-materials, contact-us, search |

**Lesson for La Rose:** the page count comes from *one page per doctor* and *one page per
specialty*, plus a genuine "plan your visit" cluster and an editorial cluster. Everything
else is a hub that lists those.

## 2. Homepage section order

1. Utility bar — group link · careers · contact · language switch
2. Header — phone number, "scan for easy booking" QR, **BOOK AN APPOINTMENT** button
3. Main nav — Specialties · Facilities · Medical Centers · Plan Your Visit · Academy · About Us · International Patient Services · search
4. **Hero carousel** — featured clinics & announcements, each with VIEW DETAILS
5. **Appointment finder** — `Select specialty → Select doctor` inline widget. The single most useful element on the page.
6. Quick-action tiles — Home Care | WhatsApp | Virtual Consultation
7. Centers of Excellence — carousel of 14 accredited centres
8. News / "Discover" — 4 news cards
9. Medical Centers — the branch network, card per branch
10. Specialties grid — 7 shown + VIEW ALL
11. **Patient testimonials** — carousel, quote + name + what they were treated for
12. Virtual tour — photo gallery of the facility
13. Accreditation & awards — logo wall (20 items). Heavy trust signalling.
14. Book Now CTA band
15. Mega footer — *every* specialty listed as a link, then section links, legal, address, phone

## 3. Specialty page anatomy

- Hero: breadcrumb (`GENERAL SURGERY > BARIATRIC SURGERY`), large title, Book CTA
- Tab bar: **OVERVIEW | DOCTORS | UPDATES**
- Long-form body, all-caps H2s:
  - Conditions for undergoing X
  - What are the benefits of X
  - What are the types of X (each type gets a sub-heading + outcome numbers + differences)
  - **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS** (4–6 Q&As)
  - Closing paragraph naming the department and its team
- Floating WhatsApp button, persistent

Body copy on the bariatric page runs ~1,400 words. This is what "rich in information for SEO"
looks like in practice — not padding, but genuinely answering the questions a patient types.

## 4. What to take, and what to do better

**Take:** the IA (one page per doctor / per specialty / per branch), the appointment finder
widget, the tabbed specialty page, the long-form + FAQ content model, the trust wall, the
mega footer, the "plan your visit" cluster.

**Do better:** Fakeeh's visual design is institutional and flat — a teal gradient hero, generic
cards, no real typographic voice. La Rose has a far stronger brand (high-contrast serif
wordmark, olive/sage + beige/rose palette, botanical motifs, arch framing) and should read as
*premium wellness clinic*, not *corporate hospital*. Warmth and craft are the differentiator.

## 5. Gaps in Fakeeh that La Rose must fill

- No reviews *per specialty* — La Rose needs this (client requirement)
- No before/after galleries — La Rose needs this per specialty where relevant
- No e-commerce / digital products — La Rose Digital Solutions needs this
- Accreditation wall assumes a large institution; La Rose substitutes real proof:
  Google 5.0 (137), ESPEN membership, InBody technology, named credentials, YouTube testimonials
