## Original coverage brief

Lead with the answer Egyptian patients need: abdominal ultrasound can suggest liver fat and may reveal other abdominal findings, but a report saying fatty liver does not by itself quantify every amount of fat, diagnose inflammation, or rule in/out fibrosis. Explain what report wording can mean, why a normal or abnormal liver-enzyme result needs context, which metabolic and alternative causes a clinician may review, and what laboratory or non-invasive fibrosis assessment may be considered. Distinguish ultrasound preparation from result interpretation, and link naturally to the existing fatty-liver fibrosis assessment, liver-test context, abdominal-ultrasound preparation, insulin-resistance and metabolic-risk pages.

Do not turn a visual ultrasound grade into a prognosis, claim that a particular scan has been personally read, or provide a universal diet or medication. Include urgent assessment boundaries such as jaundice, vomiting blood, black stool, confusion, abdominal swelling, persistent severe pain, fever with abdominal symptoms or rapidly worsening illness.
