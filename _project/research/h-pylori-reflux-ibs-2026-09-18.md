## Editorial gap and plan

The results repeatedly collapse bloating, heartburn and abdominal pain into a self-diagnosis, often with a symptom-by-symptom table. The revised article instead begins with the Egyptian reader’s real question, then separates *location and timing* from diagnosis, explains what each test is meant to answer, names alarm features, and gives a small appointment record. It explicitly avoids self-starting antibiotics, stopping acid medicine for a test without instructions, broad food restriction, and assuming that an ultrasound or a normal test diagnoses IBS. Contextual links lead only to the site’s distinct H. pylori, reflux, IBS, coeliac-testing, ultrasound and endoscopy pathways.
