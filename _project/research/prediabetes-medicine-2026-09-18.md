## Coverage gap and original angle

Results commonly turn a single HbA1c number into an automatic medication decision. The refresh explains that the number is a screening/diagnostic clue to interpret in context, separates confirming the result from assigning treatment, names the factors that can make medicine reasonable for selected higher-risk adults, and replaces generic “diet advice” with a measurable, reviewable plan. It does not prescribe or promise prevention for any individual.

## Evidence selected

ADA Standards of Care (classification/diagnosis and prevention), CDC prediabetes guidance, NIDDK testing and risk guidance, the Diabetes Prevention Program trial and outcomes study, USPSTF screening recommendation, NICE prevention guidance, and Mayo Clinic’s patient-facing overview.
