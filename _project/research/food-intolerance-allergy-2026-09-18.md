## Coverage gap and editorial approach

Search results often use a symptom table that makes “no rash = no allergy” sound definitive, or promote home elimination and testing. The rewrite leads with safety: breathing, circulation or rapid swelling after food requires emergency care. It then explains why timing, reproducibility, dose, skin/respiratory symptoms and the person’s history guide specialist testing; why an IgG panel does not diagnose food allergy; and why broad food avoidance can damage nutrition, especially in children.
