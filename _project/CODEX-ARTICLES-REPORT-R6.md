# Knowledge Centre expansion report, round 6

Date: 2026-09-11

## Coverage used to choose the topics

The regenerated input contained 342 literal keyword misses. I clustered them by meaning, then removed the location and "best doctor" searches, places the clinic does not serve, retired-device queries, and variants already answered by the 127 published entries. That discarded the broad clusters for InBody preparation and interpretation, general and sex-specific hair loss, ordinary acne home care, acne marks, hormonal acne, local fat injections, cellulite, sleeve-gastrectomy basics, insulin resistance, fatty liver, H. pylori, adult IBS, general hernia, gallstones, and abdominal ultrasound.

The remaining opportunities are narrower clinical decision questions within an offered specialty. They answer a distinct patient situation or procedure stage rather than repeating the existing overview article.

## New articles

- `folliculitis-vs-acne-itchy-bumps`: Dermatology. Captures the itchy, uniform "fungal acne" cluster; it is not a duplicate because the existing acne guide does not explain how look-alike follicular bumps change the need for diagnosis.
- `deep-painful-acne-when-to-assess`: Dermatology. Captures cystic and inflamed acne at-home-treatment searches; it is not a duplicate because it focuses on deep painful lesions, scarring risk, and why squeezing needs a different response from a general routine.
- `dark-neck-skin-during-pregnancy`: Dermatology; Internal Medicine. Captures dark-neck-in-pregnancy searches; it is not a duplicate because the existing acanthosis article does not address coordinated skin and pregnancy-context assessment.

- `endoscopy-in-children-when-considered`: Paediatrics; Gastroenterology & Hepatology. Captures child gastroscopy indications and preparation searches; it is not a duplicate because the existing child H. pylori entry explains infection assessment, not the wider endoscopy decision.
- `recurrent-tummy-aches-in-children`: Paediatrics; Gastroenterology & Hepatology; Clinical Nutrition. Captures child and infant IBS-symptom searches; it is not a duplicate because it distinguishes recurrent functional-pattern questions from constipation, growth, and warning features in children.
- `ibs-symptoms-in-pregnancy-when-to-check`: Gastroenterology & Hepatology; Internal Medicine; Clinical Nutrition. Captures pregnancy-specific IBS-symptom searches; it is not a duplicate because it addresses safe coordination of bowel symptoms, supplements, and pregnancy follow-up.
- `after-gallbladder-surgery-recovery-warning-signs`: General Surgery; Gastroenterology & Hepatology; Ultrasound. Captures laparoscopic-gallbladder recovery, timing, and aftercare queries; it is not a duplicate because the existing article compares laparoscopic and open routes before surgery.
- `hernia-after-pregnancy-or-c-section`: General Surgery. Captures abdominal-wall and post-caesarean hernia-symptom searches; it is not a duplicate because the existing adult hernia guide does not address changes around pregnancy and a surgical scar.
- `gallbladder-pain-in-pregnancy-when-to-assess`: General Surgery; Gastroenterology & Hepatology; Ultrasound. Captures gallbladder-surgery-in-pregnancy searches; it is not a duplicate because it frames diagnosis and any next step around pregnancy coordination rather than the standard gallstone pathway.

## Content safeguards applied

- Each addition is a published article with five sections, three FAQ entries, a reused on-disk article image, and only valid live specialty slugs.
- Arabic is Egyptian, masculine or neutral in direct address, with no numerals in Arabic prose.
- Every article includes concrete warning signs in a dedicated "when to see a doctor" section. Byline assignments remain covered by the existing file-level `PROVISIONAL` note.
