## Scope and method

This queue covers active Knowledge Centre loader records with `type` equal to `article`, `qa`, or `tip`. `update` is a separate content type and is outside this requested queue. Word counts include excerpt, section headings/bodies, and FAQ questions/answers in each language; source labels and SEO metadata are excluded. GSC metrics sum the Arabic and English clean and `.html` URL forms, with average position weighted by impressions. Explicit section-level internal links are counted per language, excluding booking. Category reviewer coverage is based only on published, non-sample staff in `content/doctors.json`.

The supplied `Queries.csv` is a site-wide query export with no page/landing-URL column. Per-entry top-three queries therefore cannot be attributed from the supplied data; every row says `unavailable` rather than guessing from wording. `_project/GSC-SNAPSHOT-2026-09-16.json` is also site-wide and does not supply a page-query join.

Priority order is: any not-indexed flag or positive impressions first; inside that tier, impressions descending; then measurable gap count descending. `priorityScore = 1,000,000,000 + impressions × 1,000 + gap points` for the search-signal tier, otherwise the gap-point count.
