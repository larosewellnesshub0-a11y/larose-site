The La Rose Recipe Book

One question comes up again and again with any nutrition plan: what do I eat today? This book answers it with real Egyptian food, ingredients found in any kitchen, and calculated calories.

What is inside?

  • 250 Egyptian recipes across breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
  • Calories and macros worked out for every recipe
  • Ingredients from any supermarket, nothing imported
  • A substitution for each recipe when an ingredient is unavailable
  • Batch-preparation ideas for the week
  • A PDF that works on both phone and laptop

Price is set after the consultation
