Technology and equipment

Every device has a defined role in the plan

Equipment is not a plan by itself. Its use is decided after assessment, with the role of each measurement or session explained for your case.

Within the consultation

Measurements and examination that complete the picture

InBody

InBody body-composition analysis

The consultation is more than a scale reading. We run an InBody body-composition analysis to see your fat, muscle and water distribution rather than just total weight, and we go through your medical history and your labs. We will not recommend something you do not need; if a different route suits your case better, we will say so.

In the same visit

General internal medicine consultation

The consultation starts with a full history and the medicines you are actually taking, then a clinical examination and a review of every lab and scan you bring, however old. If the case calls for an abdominal ultrasound it is done in the same visit. You leave with a written plan: the treatment, the investigations needed, and when to come back.

Body contouring

The four available contouring treatments

Lost weight but your abdomen looks the same? That is not a coincidence. Your body decides where it loses fat from first and usually leaves the stubborn areas until last, which is why someone can lose ten kilos and stay the same dress size. Mesotherapy works on those areas specifically, but it is not a substitute for weight loss: it is a stage that comes after it or alongside it, and it starts with a consultation that decides whether it suits your case at all.

Localised injections for stubborn fat

Mesotherapy

Injections placed directly into the fat layer under the skin, so the treatment works on exactly the area that bothers you rather than on the whole body: abdomen, flanks and upper arms. It is done as weekly sessions, and most cases need four to six. The consultation comes first: your medical history, your medicines, the condition of the skin at the site and your weight-loss plan all decide whether mesotherapy suits you and which areas are actually worth treating.
