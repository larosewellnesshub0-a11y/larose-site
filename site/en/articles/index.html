Today's practical tip

Put a note on the freezer listing three complete pairings made from what is actually inside, such as frozen vegetables with chicken and bread, or lentil soup with yoghurt. Cross one off when used and refresh the list after shopping. Seeing a whole meal is quicker than searching drawer by drawer and makes an impulsive order less likely at the end of a long day.
