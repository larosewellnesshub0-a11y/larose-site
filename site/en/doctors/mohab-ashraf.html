About the doctor

Dr. Mohab Ashraf Fouad is a specialist in internal medicine and diabetes, and in hepatology, gastroenterology and endoscopy, with an MSc in General Internal Medicine, Hepatology, Gastroenterology and Endoscopy and more than six years of experience in diagnosing and treating internal, liver and digestive conditions. He performs the clinical examination and the abdominal and pelvic ultrasound in the same visit, reading the scan himself in light of the symptoms and medical history, which saves time and supports a more accurate diagnosis. He follows stomach, colon, liver and gallbladder conditions, as well as diabetes, blood pressure and thyroid disorders, with a clear treatment plan and regular follow-up.
