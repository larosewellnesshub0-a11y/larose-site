Growth and nutrition follow-up from day one

Paediatrics at La Rose covers routine growth monitoring, vaccination and common childhood illnesses. It also works alongside the nutrition department for cases that need a specialised nutrition plan, including underweight children, childhood obesity, poor appetite and food allergy.

Conditions we treat

  • Growth, weight and height monitoring
  • Routine and optional vaccinations
  • Colds and respiratory allergy
  • Colic, constipation and digestive problems in children
  • Poor appetite and underweight children
  • Obesity in children and adolescents
  • Food allergy
  • Anaemia and vitamin D deficiency
  • Newborn follow-up

Treatments & services

Growth monitoring

Against standard growth charts, not by impression

Weight, height and head circumference measured and plotted on standard growth charts, so we can see whether the child is following their own normal trajectory, not a comparison with another child. It is deviation from the curve that warrants investigation, not the number itself.

Children's nutrition

With a clinical nutrition specialist experienced in children's nutrition

Poor appetite and being underweight in children is not always behavioural: anaemia, zinc deficiency or a food intolerance can sit behind it. The plan is built after examination and blood work, and is designed to be something a child will actually follow rather than an ideal chart.

Vaccination

The routine and optional schedules

Following the routine vaccination schedule, and discussing the optional vaccines, which of them suit your child and when. If there has been a delay or missed doses, we review the schedule with you.
