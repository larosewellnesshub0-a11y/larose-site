Body Contouring

Injections for stubborn localised fat, with no surgery and no downtime

Book an appointment WhatsApp

Lost weight but your abdomen looks the same? That is not a coincidence. Your body decides where it loses fat from first and usually leaves the stubborn areas until last, which is why someone can lose ten kilos and stay the same dress size. Mesotherapy works on those areas specifically, but it is not a substitute for weight loss: it is a stage that comes after it or alongside it, and it starts with a consultation that decides whether it suits your case at all.

Conditions we treat

  • Stubborn abdominal and flank fat
  • Fat on the hips, thighs and upper arms
  • Back fat
  • Localised areas left behind after weight loss
  • Areas that do not respond to diet and exercise alone

Treatments & services

Mesotherapy

Localised injections for stubborn fat

Injections placed directly into the fat layer under the skin, so the treatment works on exactly the area that bothers you rather than on the whole body: abdomen, flanks and upper arms. It is done as weekly sessions, and most cases need four to six. The consultation comes first: your medical history, your medicines, the condition of the skin at the site and your weight-loss plan all decide whether mesotherapy suits you and which areas are actually worth treating.

One session per week, four to six sessions for most cases The areas and the number of injections are set at the consultation No surgery, no anaesthetic and no downtime Not suitable in pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with some medical conditions: the consultation checks this first

We review after four to six weekly sessions. The change shows in your measurements and in how clothes fit before it shows on the scale. If there is no measurable change by then, we will say so and reconsider the plan rather than sell you more sessions.

Risks depend on the substance, area and injection technique. Pain, redness, swelling or bruising may occur, as can allergy, infection, lumps or scarring. Ask about the substance, its source and alternatives, and seek prompt review for increasing pain, spreading redness, fever or discharge.

The procedure should be postponed during pregnancy. During breastfeeding, safety depends on the substance because a local injection does not mean there is no absorption. Review it with the obstetric clinician and injector, using the exact substance rather than the word mesotherapy alone.
