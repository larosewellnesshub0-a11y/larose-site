Weight Management & Non-surgical Alternatives

Weight-loss injections under medical supervision, with weekly follow-up

Book an appointment WhatsApp

Weight-loss injections are medication, not a cosmetic product, and they require careful dose adjustment and medical supervision. We start with the lowest effective dose and increase it gradually according to your response, alongside weekly follow-up and a nutrition plan, so that what you lose is fat rather than muscle, and so that it stays lost.

Conditions we treat

  • Obesity that has not responded to diet alone
  • Repeated cycles of losing weight and regaining it
  • Non-surgical alternatives to bariatric surgery
  • Obesity alongside insulin resistance
  • Obesity alongside PCOS
  • Maintaining weight after loss

Treatments & services

Medically supervised weight-loss treatment

Medically approved options, stepped gradually, with weekly follow-up

There are medically approved treatments that support weight loss by acting on the hormones that regulate hunger and fullness: they reduce appetite and slow stomach emptying, and some also help with insulin resistance. They are given as a weekly injection, starting at the lowest dose and stepping up in stages according to your response and your comfort, rather than being set once. The consultation decides whether this route suits your case at all, and which option fits you best; if it does not suit you we will say so and suggest the alternative. It always runs alongside a nutrition plan and weekly follow-up, never on its own.

Maintenance after weight loss

The stage where most people come undone

The hardest stage is not the loss, it is holding it. Once you reach your target we build a gradual maintenance plan that returns calories to a normal level without the weight returning, with longer-interval follow-up and body-composition measurement to make sure what comes back is not fat.
