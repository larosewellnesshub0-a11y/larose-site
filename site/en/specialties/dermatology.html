Dermatology & Aesthetics

Medical treatment for your skin, not generic prescriptions

Many skin and hair problems can have an internal cause, including vitamin deficiency, hormonal imbalance, insulin resistance or a thyroid disorder. That is why dermatology at La Rose works alongside the nutrition and internal medicine departments: we treat the skin directly while looking for the underlying cause at the same time.

Conditions we treat

  • Acne and acne scarring
  • Pigmentation, melasma and dark circles
  • Hair loss and alopecia
  • Eczema and psoriasis
  • Skin allergy and urticaria
  • Fungal and other skin infections
  • Warts and skin tags
  • Stretch marks after weight loss
  • Skin ageing and wrinkles

Treatments & services

Dermatology consultation

Diagnosing the cause, not just the symptom

The consultation covers examination of the skin or scalp, a review of your medical history, medications and labs, and establishing whether the problem is local or has an internal cause that needs investigating. The plan is written on that basis, not as a generic prescription.

Acne treatment programme

A staged plan, not a single treatment

Acne is not one type and not one cause. The programme begins by identifying the type and grade, checking for hormonal or dietary causes where present, then a staged topical or systemic treatment plan with follow-up, and finally a phase for treating the scarring.

Hair loss treatment

Starts with a blood test, not a product

Hair loss is a symptom, not a diagnosis. The first step here is blood work: iron and ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid function, and hormones where the case calls for it. Treating hair loss without knowing the cause wastes time and money. If the cause is nutritional, the case moves to the nutrition department to be treated at its root.
